Calamity Preparedness Drill & Guide

Know what to do before, during, and after common calamities affecting Cebu City (floods, typhoons, earthquakes, fires).

Flood / Bagyo

1
Before: Move to higher ground. Secure important documents (Valid ID, PSA, birth certificate) in a waterproof bag.
2
During: Do not walk or drive through floodwater. Avoid electrical panels and substations.
3
After: Wait for the all-clear signal. Take photos of damage for your incident report. Bring Valid ID and proof of address when reporting to barangay.

Earthquake

1
Drop, Cover, Hold On — get under a sturdy table or desk. Stay away from windows.
2
After: Evacuate if the building is damaged. Report injuries and structural damage immediately to 911.

Fire

1
Immediately call 911 or 032-253-4011 (Bureau of Fire Protection). Do not use elevators.
2
Stop, Drop, and Roll if clothing catches fire. Crawl low under smoke to avoid inhaling fumes.
3
Once safe: document property damage with photos. Keep Valid ID and Barangay clearance ready for assistance.

Medical Emergency

1
Call 911 or 032-253-1234 (ambulance). Provide exact address and nature of emergency.
2
Keep the person conscious and still. Have Valid ID and any medical records ready.
